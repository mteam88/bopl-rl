# Ability differential gaps

This queue started from the 29 `partial` and two `slice-only` entries in
`native/data/ability-coverage.json` at commit `2f3cf18`; an entry is removed when
the ability's remaining source behavior is ported wholesale and its catalog
status becomes `ported`.
Rank reflects how often the
missing branch should arise in unconstrained RL play: ordinary casts, airborne
casts and pickup-driven loadout changes come first; cross-ability contacts come
next; rare destruction, time-stop and pool-eviction boundaries come last. Each
entry names source behavior that is still missing or has not yet been audited
against an end-to-end NativeAOT/Rust differential through the public rollout
ABI. Per-branch differential coverage can trail a completed source port; that
distinction stays explicit in the catalog's verification text.

1. **ID 20 — Meteor (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. The downward ray's player and item hit callbacks, `DropAllAttachedPlayers` for riders and rope/attachment owners, and isolated water or no-hit airborne exits remain un-goldened; exact compositions with moving platforms, transformed victims, Time Stop, post-exit pickup collection and acquired-slot use are also absent.
2. **ID 16 — Roll (`partial`):** Lethal contact against a Dash ray, exact post-release stock reconstruction, charging-form Time Stop freeze/restored release, Gust's airborne PlayerPhysics force through lower-boundary death, and an immediate airborne release through `ShortJump`, descending exit and ordinary landing are covered; exact episodes are still missing for platform-to-platform hopping, turns on moving or rotating platforms, wall squish, lethal swept contact against ordinary and other transformed players, Freeze interaction, death during the form, and post-exit pickup collection and acquired-slot use.
3. **ID 14 — Platform (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. The cast and normal riding/contact path is exact, but no exact differential spans the remaining ability-body interactions: attachments from Spike/Engine/Tesla/ropes, Push/Meteor/Gust/Black Hole forces, portals and scale rays, transformed-player squish, post-exit pickup collection and acquired-slot use, or the known chaotic contact-loaded moving-platform trajectories.
4. **ID 5 — Engine (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Time Stop during live thrust, including the attached composite platform and restored force clock, is exact. Smoke ignition is implemented but still lacks an exact chain differential; four-slot ring eviction, simultaneous engines, Growth/Shrink changes, Time Stop during the short startup animation, supporting-platform destruction or Macho transfer, owner death, and post-exit pickup collection plus acquired-slot use remain un-goldened.
5. **ID 23 — Tesla coil (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Time Stop across an already-connected pair, composite/line geometry, a paused flicker and the next complete cycle is exact. Exact compositions remain missing for Growth/Shrink changes, supporting-platform destruction or Macho transfer, Time Stop during connection startup, Smoke and Mine ignition, mixed Engine/Spike/rope attachments, simultaneous victim contacts and pickup/death removal of the casting owner.
6. **ID 21 — Throw (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Consuming a platform has not been exactly composed with transfer of Spike, Engine, Tesla or rope attachments to the held boulder; boulder interactions with Magnet, Portal, Black Hole, Gust/Meteor Shockwaves, Time Stop, scale rays, transformed victims and pickup/death forced exits also lack exact differentials.
7. **ID 22 — Push (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Supporting-platform destruction, caster sudden death, post-exit pickup collection and acquired-slot use, overlapping-player contacts, moving-platform edge transitions and compositions with attached Spike/Engine/Tesla/ropes or external Gust/Meteor/Black Hole forces remain without exact differentials.
8. **ID 11 — Spike (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Recast replacement and the persistent box's player kill, team/owner attribution and transformed-form contacts are implemented but un-goldened; supporting-platform destruction, Macho transfer, Growth/Shrink, Time Stop, Shockwave/Push motion and pickup/death cleanup also lack exact compositions.
9. **ID 33 — Poke ball (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Gust force is exact on both the held form and released ball through the fixed-set force curve, and Magnet pull/pickup/pinning/fire is exact for the released ball. A crossing Bow Arrow is exact through dynamic Circle/Circle correction, Arrow destruction, Ball survival and the Ball's later lower-boundary removal. Time Stop on the held form through restored release is exact, the released ball is exact through all 690 stopped passes and restored motion, and a live capture pauses for exactly 690 passes before restored expiry/ejection, while both capture differentials retain bounded inherited player/platform drift rather than exact positions. `Item.Duplicate` copies and duplicated callback capture are exact through the first capture, a second queued capture of the already-held victim and 12 pinned passes; the multi-captor expiry/ejection boundary remains open. Exact episodes are still missing for multiple equidistant victims, owner/team rejection, Portal motion, and post-exit pickup collection and acquired-slot use while a victim remains held.
10. **ID 35 — Tack (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Gust force is exact on both the held form and released projectile through the fixed-set force curve, and Magnet pull/pickup/pinning/fire is exact for the released projectile. `Item.Duplicate` copies are exact through fresh runtime state, deferred registration, arming and consumption. A crossing Bow Arrow is exact through item-specific Circle/Circle correction, Arrow destruction, post-resolution Tack landing callbacks and continued flight without a false settle. Time Stop on the held form through restored release, the released Projectile owner, and a stop beginning 43 ticks into the 48-tick arming delay through restored owner-clear/re-entry self-kill are exact; exact compositions are still missing for Portal/Black Hole movement, Growth/Shrink, Time Stop near expiry, non-owner transformed victims, simultaneous contacts, death during the form, and post-exit pickup collection plus acquired-slot use.
11. **ID 26 — Drill (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Rope detachment on entry/burrow/exit and direct Gust/Meteor Shockwave pushing of the active form remain un-goldened, along with moving-platform tunneling, transformed-form hitbox contacts, Growth/Shrink, Portal/Black Hole interaction, owner death during a burrow, and post-exit pickup collection plus acquired-slot use.
12. **ID 15 — Revival (`partial`):** An active-form nonlethal Dash no-push callback is exact. Pickup replacement followed by immediate airborne activation of the acquired Revival, marker creation and exit is exact. The public pool still lacks an exact differential for simultaneous markers belonging to multiple live clones, copied equipped-ability state beyond the currently represented clone graph, marker interaction with Portal/Teleport/Black Hole/scale effects, and deaths during Time Stop or another active form.
