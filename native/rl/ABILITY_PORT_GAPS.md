# Ability differential gaps

This queue started from the 29 `partial` and two `slice-only` entries in
`native/data/ability-coverage.json` at commit `2f3cf18`; entries stay here while
their remaining gaps are closed and their status labels track the catalog.
Rank reflects how often the
missing branch should arise in unconstrained RL play: ordinary casts, airborne
casts and pickup-driven loadout changes come first; cross-ability contacts come
next; rare destruction, time-stop and pool-eviction boundaries come last. Each
entry names only source behavior that does not yet have an exact end-to-end
NativeAOT/Rust differential through the public rollout ABI. A branch can already
be implemented and still appear here when its dedicated differential is absent.

1. **ID 2 — Dash (`partial`):** Active-Rock and lethal-Roll deaths, ordinary-player push/pass-through, active-Bow no-push, live Web Swing bottom-endpoint transfer and Grappling Hook top-endpoint transfer are differential-covered; other transformed-player callbacks, collision-mask and hierarchy tie cases, detached-rope entry and the horizontal-under-water zero-divide failure still lack exact public-rollout episodes.
2. **ID 4 — Bow (`partial`):** Ordinary arrow flight, projectile and active-form Time Stop freeze/restoration, Magnet pull/pickup/fire, Gust capture/force, and the active Bow form's nonlethal response to an overlapping Dash ray are differential-covered, but live compositions with other projectile, item, Black Hole, Portal, transformed-form and ability colliders have not each been exercised; Unity's inert landed-arrow child retention also remains absent from the entity-history comparison.
3. **ID 7 — Gust (`slice-only`):** The player/platform cast and fixed-set force delivery to a live thrown Grenade and Bow Arrow are exact, but Shockwave capture still lacks public differentials for the other spawned items and projectiles, boulders, active forms, Black Hole, rope-attached bodies and destructible ability objects.
4. **ID 12 — Time stop (`partial`):** The base cast plus Bow arrow/form, active Rock and charging Roll freeze/restoration are exact, including Rock's repeated zero-delta scale update and Roll's restored lethal release, but freeze-entry, held-state, updater-clock and first-restored-pass behavior is still unmeasured for Portal transit, other active forms, boulders, ropes, scale effects, Tesla/Engine attachments, Black Holes and pickup collection or replacement during the stop.
5. **ID 6 — Blink gun (`partial`):** Common airborne casts and successful tunnel hits on walls, movable rigid bodies, thrown items, projectiles and orbs lack exact differentials; so do water-reflected shots, active-form victims, rope or Magnet detachment, platform selection after a pickup-changed loadout, and airborne relocation/landing publication.
6. **ID 8 — Growth ray (`partial`):** Only an ordinary grounded player hit is exact; water reflection, airborne and active-form victims, thrown items or other non-player scalable targets, Black Hole growth, orb conversion, concurrent ScaleChangers and scale changes across death, pickup replacement or another ability form remain without exact public differentials.
7. **ID 17 — Shrink ray (`partial`):** Only an ordinary grounded player hit is exact; water reflection, airborne and active-form victims, thrown items or other non-player scalable targets, Black Hole interaction, orb conversion, concurrent ScaleChangers and scale changes across death, pickup replacement or another ability form remain without exact public differentials.
8. **ID 36 — Freeze ray (`partial`):** Water reflection is implemented but un-goldened, and freezing an active Bow form currently has an unresolved original-slime restoration/updater-order mismatch; exact differentials are also missing for other active forms, airborne or rope-attached victims, repeat-freeze/cooldown reset, death while frozen and pickup-driven slot replacement during `FrozenSolid`.
9. **ID 30 — Magnet gun (`partial`):** Layer-9 Arrow pull/pickup/fire is differential-covered; other projectiles, Macho boulders, Black Holes, ragdoll players and held weapon/spell objects still lack exact compositions, as does lock loss from distance, destruction, owner death, landing or pickup slot replacement.
10. **ID 29 — Duplicator (`partial`):** Exact public compositions are missing for copying players/forms, arrows and other projectile-layer bodies, weapons/spells, Black Holes, orbs and attached terrain; the shared item-copy path still needs dedicated Smoke, Tack, Mjolnir, Poke Ball and Mine differentials, including pickup replacement and copied-object expiry.
11. **ID 3 — Grenade (`partial`):** Terrain bounce, delayed non-owner contact and owner-clear/re-entry behavior lack dedicated exact episodes, as do forced ability exits from death, pickup replacement or another form and `Item.Duplicate` copies through held, released, fuse and explosion states.
12. **ID 10 — Missile (`partial`):** Exact compositions remain missing for direct player/form, arrow/projectile and thrown-item collisions, Time Stop entry/exit, Growth/Shrink scaling, Duplicator copies, Portal traversal beyond the generic target path, boundary destruction and a dedicated victim/credit test for `MineExplosion` lethality.
13. **ID 24 — Mine (`partial`):** Projectile- and explosion-triggered ignition, Magnet pull/hold/release, `Item.Duplicate`, Growth/Shrink scaling, water/bounds removal, Time Stop at each armed/chase/explosion phase and pickup-driven forced exit have no exact end-to-end differential.
14. **ID 37 — Mjolnir (`partial`):** Player and transformed-form victim hits, owner attribution/immunity, simultaneous platform-and-victim contact, Magnet/Portal/Black Hole interactions, Time Stop during outbound or recall flight and `Item.Duplicate` copies remain outside exact public differentials.
15. **ID 9 — Rock (`partial`):** Active-form Time Stop clock, scale and restored-motion behavior is exact; exact compositions are still absent for Shockwave pushes, arrows attaching to the Rock, projectile/item detonation, Portal/Magnet/Black Hole contact, Growth/Shrink scale changes and Rock contact against other transformed player forms.
16. **ID 38 — Portal gun (`partial`):** The public golden is exact for shots, mouths and ordinary-player transit, while the Grenade composition inherits a non-exact incoming ThrowItem2 pose; exact differential episodes are still missing for arrows, pickups, Missiles, Rock, boulders, Revival/Teleport markers, other movable rounded bodies, active forms, ropes and Time Stop across capture or linked-mouth expiry.
17. **ID 25 — Teleport (`partial`):** Player area swaps are exact, but the source's same-query relocation of arrows/projectiles, thrown items, pickups, Missiles, Rock/Macho boulders and other movable ability bodies has not been covered by exact public compositions, including mixed-team overlap and Time Stop at either cast.
18. **ID 18 — Black hole (`partial`):** Standalone arrows/projectiles, thrown items, pickups, Missiles, boulders and other ability bodies still lack exact attraction, center-consumption, owner-credit and growth-reset compositions; multiple-hole merging with unequal mass, negative/orb-produced holes and Time Stop also lack dedicated public differentials.
19. **ID 20 — Meteor (`partial`):** The downward ray's player and item hit callbacks, `DropAllAttachedPlayers` for riders and rope/attachment owners, and isolated water or no-hit airborne exits remain un-goldened; exact compositions with moving platforms, transformed victims, Time Stop and pickup replacement during charge are also absent.
20. **ID 16 — Roll (`partial`):** Lethal contact against a Dash ray, exact post-release stock reconstruction and charging-form Time Stop freeze/restored release are covered; exact episodes are still missing for platform-to-platform hopping, immediate airborne release, turns on moving or rotating platforms, wall squish, lethal swept contact against ordinary and other transformed players, Shockwave/Freeze interaction and forced exit after pickup replacement or death.
21. **ID 14 — Platform (`partial`):** The cast and normal riding/contact path is exact, but no exact differential spans the remaining ability-body interactions: attachments from Spike/Engine/Tesla/ropes, Push/Meteor/Gust/Black Hole forces, portals and scale rays, transformed-player squish, pickup replacement during the form, or the known chaotic contact-loaded moving-platform trajectories.
22. **ID 5 — Engine (`partial`):** Smoke ignition is implemented but still lacks an exact chain differential; four-slot ring eviction, simultaneous engines, Growth/Shrink changes, Time Stop during startup/thrust, supporting-platform destruction or Macho transfer, and pickup/death removal remain un-goldened.
23. **ID 23 — Tesla coil (`partial`):** Exact compositions remain missing for Growth/Shrink changes, supporting-platform destruction or Macho transfer, Time Stop across connection/flicker, Smoke and Mine ignition, mixed Engine/Spike/rope attachments, simultaneous victim contacts and pickup/death removal of the casting owner.
24. **ID 13 — Smoke (`partial`):** Ignition by Engine, Grenade, Mine and other smoke clouds, recursive chain timing and owner credit lack exact differentials; Growth/Shrink changes, repeated-cast 40-cloud ring eviction, Time Stop, Magnet/Portal motion and `Item.Duplicate` also remain un-goldened.
25. **ID 21 — Throw (`partial`):** Consuming a platform has not been exactly composed with transfer of Spike, Engine, Tesla or rope attachments to the held boulder; boulder interactions with Magnet, Portal, Black Hole, Gust/Meteor Shockwaves, Time Stop, scale rays, transformed victims and pickup/death forced exits also lack exact differentials.
26. **ID 22 — Push (`partial`):** Supporting-platform destruction, caster sudden death or pickup replacement while channeling, overlapping-player contacts, moving-platform edge transitions and compositions with attached Spike/Engine/Tesla/ropes or external Gust/Meteor/Black Hole forces remain without exact differentials.
27. **ID 11 — Spike (`partial`):** Recast replacement and the persistent box's player kill, team/owner attribution and transformed-form contacts are implemented but un-goldened; supporting-platform destruction, Macho transfer, Growth/Shrink, Time Stop, Shockwave/Push motion and pickup/death cleanup also lack exact compositions.
28. **ID 33 — Poke ball (`partial`):** The capture differential has bounded inherited player/platform drift rather than exact positions; exact episodes remain missing for multiple equidistant victims, owner/team/invulnerability rejection, boundary removal, Time Stop during capture or ejection, Magnet/Portal motion, `Item.Duplicate` copies and pickup replacement while a victim is held.
29. **ID 35 — Tack (`partial`):** `Item.Duplicate` is the explicit uncovered source branch; exact compositions are also missing for Magnet/Portal/Black Hole movement, Growth/Shrink, Time Stop across arming/expiry, non-owner transformed victims, simultaneous contacts and pickup/death forced exit while the item is held.
30. **ID 26 — Drill (`partial`):** Rope detachment on entry/burrow/exit and direct Gust/Meteor Shockwave pushing of the active form remain un-goldened, along with moving-platform tunneling, transformed-form hitbox contacts, Growth/Shrink, Portal/Black Hole interaction and pickup replacement or owner death during a burrow.
31. **ID 15 — Revival (`partial`):** The public pool represents one live marker per player; exact differentials are still missing for simultaneous markers belonging to multiple live clones, copied equipped-ability state beyond the currently represented clone graph, marker interaction with Portal/Teleport/Black Hole/scale effects, pickup slot replacement and deaths during Time Stop or another active form.
