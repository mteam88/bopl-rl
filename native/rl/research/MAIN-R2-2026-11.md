## 3. Round 2 (pre-registered; train/research/arms/main-r2.toml, analyze.py `MAIN_R2`)

**Design.** 2^2 full factorial, 2 replicates, 8 runs (seeds 601-608, shuffled order_seed 2), each 6B constant LR
(EMA half-life 5B) + the matched 1B anneal to 1.2e-5 from l2-main-136002m, observation v4.2, held-out panel
excluded, 4300 panel games per endpoint on base 211001 (E0/off1's setups), the on-kit exploit panel in slots 8-11.
Trainer determinism flags (`BOPL_GEMM_TUNE=0 BOPL_V4_LT_TUNE=0`) are **not** used: replicated seeds already carry
the nondeterminism and the arms must stay comparable with the five scored base replicates trained without them;
the flags stay for bit-off build gates only.

- **D, I1 dose:** premium 0.5 (styles/offense_kill_05.toml, the adopted level, low) vs 1.0 (styles/offense_kill_10.toml,
  high). Primary: KH_PRE (eval/kill_rate.py, game credit) at the annealed endpoint, 4 high runs vs 4 low.
  Adopt 1.0 if ratio >= 1.10 with the seed-bootstrap 95% CI above 1, the panel guard holds and no veto.
- **X, drill exposure:** low = the base league. High = `--bank-groups all,all,pinned,forgotten --pinned-bank
  R2-s7211,R2x2,l2-me31-drill-grenade-2000m,x2-drill-1500m` with R2-s7211 and R2x2 added to `--opponents`
  (league members since 2026-11-01, kind exploiter, Drill/Dash/Dash kits). `--pinned-bank` is new (train.py,
  native_selfplay.candidates): the pinned bank draws only that sub-list and only the sub-list leaves the other
  groups, so the other ~30 kit-pinned exploiters stay ordinary PFSP candidates on their own kits and the rest of
  the mix is the base's. Dose: bank 3 of 4 is about a quarter of the historical games, ~8% of learner rows
  (historical-every 2), versus x2d's 100%. Primary: score vs **R2-s7212** on its kits (held out: never a training
  opponent at either level; 800 games, se ~0.018), 4 high vs 4 low. Adopt if high - low >= +0.05 with one-sided
  pooled-t p < 0.05 (df 6, per-run sd floored at the eval se 0.018), the panel guard holds, KH_PRE high/low >= 0.90
  and no veto. x2-drill-kit, l2-me31-kit and R2-s7211-kit are in-distribution for X-high and report only.
- **Guard (both factors):** held-out panel non-inferiority, one-sided 95% Welch lower bound > -0.02 over seed
  means (kill_rate.MARGIN); vetoes ckpm_b up > 50% and env_loss up > 0.03 (kill_rate.py).
- **Interaction DX:** reported on panel and log KH_PRE with the replicate pure error (df 4), no decision. If both
  factors pass, the long run takes both (the DX cell is the point check that they do not cancel: if DX's panel
  mean is below ff-1's by more than 0.02 on the point, take only the factor with the larger primary effect).
- **E8 trigger:** both panel main effects (high - low) below +0.02 -> "null on strength" -> E8 after the confirm.
- **Power** (E0 s_seed 0.0078, CRN eval se 0.006 at 4300 games): a 4 vs 4 panel contrast has se ~0.007, MDE 0.02 at
  80%; the NI guard at -0.02 has power 0.83 at a true 0 (analysis.txt power table, "4v4"). KH_PRE: off1 found
  x1.27 at 16 vs 16 with CI half-width ~0.05; at 4 vs 4 expect ~0.10, so a dose effect of x1.10 is at the edge
  and x1.20 is well powered. Drill: 4 vs 4 at se 0.018 per run gives MDE ~0.045; x2d's iid +0.40 at 100% dose
  makes +0.05 at 8% dose a conservative bar.
- **Cost:** 56B, 24 GPU-h planned (18.5 measured); evals ~17k games per run, ~0.4 h wall each at 6 workers.

## 5. The long run E10 and the release

- **Config:** the confirmed flags from analyze.py's `long_run_flags` (the base + I1 at the confirmed premium +
  wd 1.5e-4 [+ the drill bank]), init l2-main-136002m (not a round-2 endpoint: a full-LR restart from an annealed
  net loses the anneal and a 6B head start is 3 GPU-h), `--ema-decay` at the 5B half-life, checkpoints every
  10B, metrics every 10M. Length 300B (129 GPU-h) with a hard stop at 11-14 00:00.
- **Gating every 20B** (CPU, overlapping): EMA weights, 4300 panel games on base 221001, the exploit panel and
  R2-s7212-kit, kill_rate.py's human-regime row. Kill rule: if the EMA panel falls > 0.03 below the best gate at
  two consecutive gates, stop, and the release candidate is the best gate's EMA weights annealed 1B. Stall rule:
  none (the loop's slope was +0.005 per 10B; the run's value is the gates, not the endpoint).
- **Endpoint:** the pre-registered 1B cosine anneal to 1.2e-5 (anneal-ctl 2026-10-21: 2B and the lower floor add
  nothing), from the last raw checkpoint or the best gate's raw checkpoint if the kill rule fired.
- **Release gate** (base 221001, 10k panel games, scored alongside the current release l2-anneal-2 on the same
  setups): (1) KH_PRE >= 1.20 x l2-anneal-2's; (2) panel one-sided 95% LB > l2-anneal-2 - 0.02 (l2-anneal-2
  trained against the panel members, so this bar favours it); (3) R2-s7212-kit and x2-drill-kit >= l2-anneal-2's
  - 0.05; (4) Matthew's playtest (1 hour, human-mac/games.jsonl) is recorded on the card and is his call. Fallback
  if (1)-(3) fail: the best gated EMA checkpoint annealed; if none passes, the confirm's best arm anneal.
- **Counterfactual** (PLAN E10): the old loop's slope predicts +0.07 per 150B before its anneal; report the long
  run's gate curve against it.

## 7. Risks to 11-18

- A crash or sim panic in round 2 (off1 had one, ff-abcdef) costs a 3 GPU-h rerun; the queue reruns a failed
  arm once (arm.py restarts an incomplete stage).
- The `--pinned-bank` path is new code: unit-tested (train/test_native_selfplay.py) and smoke-trained 120M steps
  (RL/sweeps/main-r2-smoke in M); the queue repeats a 20M-step smoke as a gate before the first arm.
- A v4.3 opponent in the bank sets BOPL_RL_FORM_BODY=1 for the X-high runs (train.py: the sim exports the Drill
  form body for the v4.3 writer). The v4.2 learner's writer does not read it (no FORM_BODY reference in
  bopl_rows_v4.h / bopl_v4.cu), but it is part of the treatment; R2v42 in drill round 2 trained a v4.2 net under it.
- If both D and X pass and the DX cell shows a cost, the long run takes one factor (Section 3): no re-run.
- The long run's hard stop protects the release window; the only way to miss 11-18 is a GPU/host outage of
  more than ~6 days after 11-06.
