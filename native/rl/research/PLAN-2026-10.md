## Execution order: Karpathy's recipe (added 2026-10-18, Matthew: "follow it as closely as possible")

Source: karpathy.github.io/2019/04/25/recipe. Training fails silently, so go simple -> complex,
make one change at a time, and check each against a concrete prediction. Sections 2-4 below
keep the measurement rules and the experiment specs; this section sets the order. No stage
starts until the previous stage's checks pass or their failure is understood.

**Stage 1. Become one with the data (CPU, days 1-2).** In RL the data is the games.
- 1a Watch games: render >= 30 full games to GIF/frames (main vs f5, main vs main, main vs the
  drill-grenade and missile exploiters, main losses to rusher-hard) and read them by eye. Hand-label
  100 random main losses into failure categories, then count each category automatically.
- 1b Distributions and outliers: per-game rows (map, kits, length, death cause, winner) for 4000+
  games. Plot length, death-cause and per-map/per-kit score distributions; inspect the extremes
  (longest/shortest games, maps or kits near 0 or 1). Outcome-noise study: same setup x K>=8
  resamples.
- 1c Visualize just before the net: decode the exact observation tensor the policy gets for
  sampled frames and compare it to the rendered frame (9-tick delay, 6-slot entity truncation,
  side mirroring, coordinate frames, ability ids).

**Stage 2. Trust the skeleton and baselines (days 2-4; GPU only for 2a and 2d).**
- 2a Fixed seed: two 50M-step runs with the same seed and init. Identical, or how far apart?
  Nondeterminism is part of seed variance and must be known before E0.
- 2b Significant digits in eval: one seed base (211001), >= 4000 paired games per decision,
  per-game rows plus eval/paired.py (Section 2).
- 2c Baselines and input-independent baselines on the same panel: random, scripted bots, and the
  main with input groups zeroed at eval (opponent, projectiles, map/rays, own state, ability ids).
  An input whose removal costs nothing is an input the net ignores.
- 2d Overfit one batch: fine-tune the main on one (map, kit pair) against one frozen opponent
  (the drill-grenade exploiter on its own kit) for <= 1B. Prediction: fast climb well above
  control. Failing that, either the learner is broken or the matchup is lost on kit alone; tell
  them apart with the mirror (same kit both sides).
- 2e Prediction dynamics: on a fixed bank of ~10k states, measure action-distribution KL and
  argmax agreement between consecutive l2 checkpoints (and later against EMA weights). Large
  jitter with no drift is the constant-LR noise floor (tests the E1/E2 hypothesis directly).
- 2f Chart dependencies with backprop: d(logits of agent i, time t) / d(obs of agent j, or time
  > t) must be 0; the actor must not depend on privileged critic inputs. Custom CUDA can mix rows
  silently.
- 2g Loss and reward sanity: reward components per game, value-target range vs the value head,
  advantage normalisation, entropy per head against its maximum.

**Stage 3. Overfit: capacity and optimizer (weeks 1-2, GPU).** E0 seed variance first. Then
"Adam is safe": an Adam arm (3e-4, and the LR matched to Muon's step size) against Muon, since
Muon is an exotic optimizer for PPO and its fixed update norm could set the noise floor. Constant
LR, with decay tuned only at the end (the recipe agrees with E1 and the matched anneal). E2 EMA.
Capacity (E8) if the net underfits its own training mix.

**Stage 4. Regularize/generalize.** Check overfitting first: score on held-out maps 3 and 4 vs
training maps, and against opponents not in the pool. Only if there is a gap, consider more
data diversity (E3 data mix). Otherwise skip this stage.

**Stage 5. Tune.** Random search (not grid) over the knobs that survive stages 3-4 (LR, entropy,
gamma, lambda, minibatch), with trial counts and games sized by E0.

**Stage 6. Squeeze out the juice.** Ensembles: average the logits of the top 3-5 nets at play time
and measure on the panel; distill the ensemble into one net; the E10 long run ("leave it
training"); final anneal and release.

## Revisions adopted 2026-10-18 from the red-team review (REVIEW-2026-10-cursor.md)

These override the sections below where they conflict.

1. **Held-out panel.** f5, A20, team5b-6250m and g111-20b-26027m are all in the l2 main's `--opponents`, and PFSP up-weights them, so a score against them is a training target. Every program arm drops all four from `--opponents`. The primary metric becomes the mean score against those four, with seed base 211001 for screens and 221001 for confirmation and release. Past l2 gates against f5, including both anneals, are in-distribution.
2. **E0 at full length.** E0 is 6 control seeds at 6B + 1B anneal, the same length as every arm. Later runs are sized from the 80% upper bound of the seed sd.
3. **One absolute anneal floor.** Every arm anneals to LR 1.2e-5, not 0.05× its own peak.
4. **Factorial instead of one-at-a-time screens.** Replace E1, E3, E4, E5 and E6 with one 16-run 2-level fractional factorial over LR, gamma, entropy, exploiter cap and minibatch, with EMA on in every run. A factor is adopted at Benjamini-Hochberg q 0.10 with an effect of at least +0.02, then confirmed with 4 vs 4 seeds on fresh eval seeds. Single-seed screens against one control seed give about 16% false promotions per null arm.
5. **Obs and reward fix first.** The v4 obs and kill-credit fix is A/B'd 4 vs 4 seeds before E0, and if it wins it becomes the base for everything after.
6. **Offense arm (E-SD).** A sudden-death start-state curriculum, plus passivity and offense metrics (time to first hit, sudden-death rate, idle rate, unforced deaths) on every report. Stage 1: 55% of games reach sudden death and 51% of losing deaths are unforced.
7. **Eval variance.** The review measured residual sd 0.019 on 400-game gates, below the binomial 0.025, so side-swapped pairs may already cancel setup variance (about 1.7× fewer games needed). Keep 4300 games per arm until E0's repeated evals confirm it.
8. **Release card.** Add exploitability by a fresh exploiter and a small human playtest. Early kill only on divergence for factorial cells, with a futility check at 3B for one-off arms.

## 2. Measurement protocol for everything below

- Primary metric: all-kit paired score vs f5 (S/final-5/run/checkpoints/policy-18666684416.bin)
  via T/league/all_combos.py, fixed seed base 211001 for the whole program (never compare across
  seed bases: Section 0 row 5). Secondary panel on the same seeds: A20
  (S/final-1/anneal4b/checkpoints/policy-26666729472.bin) and l2-main-133336m
  (L/main/checkpoints/policy-133336268800.bin); exploitability panel: x2-drill-1500m,
  x2-missile-1500m, x2-blinkplat-1500m, l2-me32-trap-2000m (200 pairs each, report only).
- Endpoint definition: every arm ends with the same matched 1B cosine anneal
  (`--anneal-lr 1 --min-lr-ratio 0.05 --steps 1000000000 --init-weights <arm last> --seed <same>`),
  from the arm's own peak LR. This holds the one-off denoising gain constant. Both the raw
  endpoint (pooled last 2 checkpoints, see next bullet) and the annealed endpoint are scored;
  the annealed one decides.
- Pooling: consecutive checkpoints of one run are indistinguishable at the gate's resolution
  (Section 0 row 2), so the raw endpoint pools the last 2 checkpoints (2 x 1000 games) at no
  GPU cost. E0 verifies this within seed.
- Per-game rows: add `--games-out F` to all_combos.py (write the `played` list, ~6 lines after
  `league_games.play`) so arms on the same seed base can be differenced pair-by-pair with
  T/eval/paired.py. E0 measures the pairing variance ratio r = var(paired diff)/var(unpaired);
  all later n are divided by 1/r (expect r 0.7-0.9 at p ~ 0.5; polish-2 saw 0.7 at p 0.98).
- CPU cost: 2000 games = 0.5 h at 6 workers (3 worker-hours), competing with the trainer's 256
  env workers (~5 cores). Run evals in `systemd-run --user --unit=eval-<x> --collect
  -p CPUWeight=30 -p MemoryHigh=8G`, 6 workers; expect ~10% trainer slowdown while they run.
  CPU budget per arm (standard kit): raw 2000 + annealed 4000 + secondary 2 x 1000 + exploit 4 x 400
  = 9600 games = 2.4 h at 6 workers.
- Power with training-seed sd s_seed, k seeds per arm, n games per seed:
  se(arm) = sqrt(s_seed^2/k + 0.25/(k n)); MDE(two arms) = 2.8 sqrt(2) se(arm).
  Examples at n = 4000: s_seed 0.01 -> k 3 gives MDE 0.029; s_seed 0.02 -> k 3 gives 0.049,
  k 8 gives 0.030; s_seed 0.03 -> k 3 gives 0.070. The whole program hinges on s_seed: E0 first.
- Screening then confirmation: screens are 1 seed, MDE 0.06 at the standard kit, meant to find
  effects >= 0.05 and kill arms at <= 0; confirmation is 3 seeds x arm + 3 shared control seeds,
  n from E0. Learning-curve slopes are not used for level questions (the anneal makes level a
  step function); they are reported for the LR arms only (E1), where rate is the question.
- Every experiment: arms share init l2-main-133336m (or the newest loop main at launch, fixed for
  the whole program), the frozen opponent list from L/main/config.json (`--opponents` exactly as
  the loop's turn 100 command, no new members), `--bank-groups all,all,all,forgotten --pfsp-p 3`,
  all other flags from L/main/config.json. Screen length 6B constant + 1B anneal = 7B = 3.0 GPU-h.
  Controls are shared across experiments (one control per seed).

## 3. Experiments, in execution order

### E0. Training-seed variance and metric calibration (week 1, day 1-2)
- Hypothesis: s_seed of the annealed endpoint is <= 0.015 (then 3-seed confirmations resolve 0.03).
- Falsified if: s_seed >= 0.03 (then only effects >= 0.07 are testable with 3 seeds; program
  shrinks to E2 and E3 with 6 seeds each).
- Design: 6 seeds (301-306) x [2B constant at l2 config -> 1B matched anneal]. Score per seed:
  raw pooled last 2 checkpoints 2000 g, annealed 4000 g, all vs f5 on base 211001 with
  `--games-out`. Also score A20 1000 g per seed.
- Outputs: s_seed (raw, annealed; chi-square CI with 5 df); anneal effect with n 6 paired
  replicates (confirms or revises +0.05 at ± 0.02); pairing ratio r; pooling check
  (checkpoint 1.33B vs 2B within seed, expect |diff| < 0.02).
- Cost: 6 x 3B x 0.43 = 7.7 GPU-h; eval 6 x 7000 = 42k games = 10.5 CPU-h (6 workers).
- Decision: s_seed <= 0.015 -> k 3, n 4000; 0.015-0.03 -> k 4-6 or n 8000; > 0.03 -> see above.

### E2. EMA weights (code first, zero GPU; week 1 day 1 engineering, then rides on every run)
- Hypothesis (noise floor): Muon at constant LR random-walks around a better policy; an EMA of
  the weights with half-life ~0.5B learner steps scores >= raw + 0.03 on constant-LR runs and
  within 0.02 of the annealed endpoint.
- Falsified if: EMA - raw < +0.015 at n 4000 paired (same run, so s_seed cancels: MDE 0.031
  unpaired, better paired). Then the anneal gain is not denoising and E1 is deprioritised.
- Change (~60 lines, rebuild with `T/train/build.py --device cuda`, then
  `T/train/check_rollout_paths.py`): in pufferlib.cu add `FloatTensor ema_weights` to PuffeRL
  (allocate next to master_weights, init = copy); after `muon_step(...)` at pufferlib.cu:1698
  launch `ema[i] = d*ema[i] + (1-d)*master[i]` on the same stream (it is captured into the CUDA
  graph, so d is a compile-time hyper: pass via hypers); add `save_ema`/`load_ema` bindings
  cloned from save_weights/save_momentum in bindings.cu and the patch; train.py:1007 writes
  `policy-<step>-ema.bin` beside each checkpoint and saves/loads `pool/ema-<step>.bin` on
  resume (as momentum, lines 1017-1036). Decays: 0.99997 (half-life 20k optimizer steps = 0.5B
  learner steps at 40.7k steps/B) and 0.99999 (1.7B). Flag `--ema-decay`.
- Protocol: every E0/E1/E3-E6 run emits EMA checkpoints; score EMA vs raw vs annealed on the same
  seeds. Primary contrast: EMA@6B - raw@6B (paired within run).
- Cost: 1 engineering day; GPU 0; eval +4000 games per run (+1 CPU-h).
- Decision: EMA >= raw + 0.03 and >= annealed - 0.02 -> ship EMA as the gated/released weights in
  the loop (gate the EMA file). EMA >= annealed + 0.02 -> EMA replaces end anneals.

### E1. LR level with matched anneals (week 1)
- Hypothesis: the constant-LR level is set by update noise; halving LR raises the raw level but
  the annealed level is unchanged (noise-limited, not basin-limited). Alternative: annealed level
  rises with lower LR by >= 0.03 (the run is hopping basins; lower the loop LR).
- Falsified (noise-only) if: annealed(1.2e-4) - annealed(2.4e-4) >= 0.03; falsified (LR matters
  at all) if raw levels are equal within 0.02.
- Arms: lr 2.43e-4 (shared control), 1.21e-4, 6.07e-5; 6B constant + 1B anneal, 1 seed screen.
  Report learning-curve slopes (checkpoints every 0.67B, 1000 g each, 9 points) because rate is
  the question for the LR arms.
- Cost: 2 arms x 3.0 = 6 GPU-h (+3 control shared); eval 2 x (9000 + 4000 + 2000) = 30k games = 7.5 CPU-h.
- Decision: pick the LR whose annealed endpoint is highest if >= +0.03 over control, confirm with
  3 seeds (9 GPU-h); otherwise keep 2.43e-4 and rely on E2/E7 (EMA, periodic anneals).

### E3. Data mix: cap the exploiter share (week 1)
- Hypothesis: 60% of PFSP games against exploiters the main cannot beat (score 0.13-0.22,
  kit-intrinsic) is low-signal data; halving that share raises general strength >= 0.03 at a
  cost <= 0.05 against the exploiter panel.
- Falsified if: annealed vs f5 within ± 0.02 of control, or exploit panel drops > 0.10.
- Arms: (a) `--bank-groups league,league,all,forgotten` (two banks exclude exploiters);
  (b) `--pfsp-p 1` with control groups; (c) `--scripted-share-min 0 --scripted-share-max 0`
  (drops rusher, 2.3% of games, tests that it matters at all; cheap negative control).
- Cost: 3 x 3.0 = 9 GPU-h; eval 3 x 9600 = 29k games = 7 CPU-h.
- Decision: (a) or (b) >= +0.03 -> confirm 3 seeds (9 GPU-h) and change l2.toml. (c) is
  informational; if (c) >= control, drop the scripted bank.

### E4. Credit assignment: gamma and lambda (week 1-2)
- Hypothesis: gamma 0.995 (half-life 138 decisions = 9 s of an 83 s game; terminal ± 1.29 reward
  is discounted to 0.002 at game start) under-credits positional play; gamma 0.998 (half-life
  35 s) raises the annealed level >= 0.03 without EV collapse.
- Falsified if: both gamma arms <= control + 0.01, or explained_variance falls below 0.80 and
  value_loss doubles (critic cannot fit the longer horizon).
- Arms: `--gamma 0.998`, `--gamma 0.999`, `--gamma 0.998 --gae-lambda 0.95`.
  Note the matched anneal also re-fits the critic at the new horizon.
- Cost: 3 x 3.0 = 9 GPU-h; eval 7 CPU-h. Confirm winner 3 seeds: 9 GPU-h.

### E5. Entropy on the discrete heads (week 2)
- Hypothesis: buttons entropy 0.045 nats and hold 0.015 nats are near-deterministic; 4-10x
  entropy on those heads finds better button/hold policies (>= +0.03 annealed).
- Falsified if: both arms <= control + 0.01 raw and annealed.
- Arms: `--entropy 0.002 --entropy-floor 0.002` and `--entropy 0.005 --entropy-floor 0.005`
  (head scales unchanged: aim gets 5.8e-5 / 1.4e-4, buttons 3e-3 / 7.5e-3).
- Cost: 2 x 3.0 = 6 GPU-h; eval 5 CPU-h.

### E6. Update granularity (week 2, lowest priority of the screens)
- Hypothesis: minibatch_div 4 (4 steps of 12k decisions) or 1 (one step of 49k) changes the
  noise/progress trade-off by >= 0.03 annealed. Prior: p3 passes gave nothing (Section 1).
- Arms: `--minibatch-div 1`, `--minibatch-div 4`. Cost: 6 GPU-h; eval 5 CPU-h.
- Falsified if: both within ± 0.02 of control. Expected outcome: null; included because it is
  the cheapest remaining optimizer knob and has never been tested.

### E7. Loop hygiene (decisions, not experiments; apply when the loop resumes)
- Drop league-exploiter turns from the rotation (0/16 added, 11% of compute).
- Gate pooling: report the mean of the last 3 gates (1200 games, MDE 0.025 vs reference level)
  instead of single 400-game gates; or raise gate_pairs to 500 (+0.25 CPU-h per gate).
- Periodic anneal-and-continue: if E2 shows EMA ~ annealed, gate/release EMA weights and never
  anneal mid-run; else every ~20B run a 1B anneal, release it, and continue from the pre-anneal
  weights at half LR (polish-2 half: +0.038, 1 seed, needs E1 confirmation first).

### E8. Capacity via distillation (week 3, conditional)
- Run only if E1+E2 show the policy is not noise-limited (EMA and lower LR gains < 0.02) or if
  >= 60 GPU-h remain after confirmations.
- Hypothesis: a 512-wide (or 2-layer) net distilled from the main (T/train/distill.py on recorded
  main games, or T/train/surgery.py widening) then trained 10B + 1B anneal beats the 256 control
  trained the same 11B by >= 0.04.
- Falsified if: within ± 0.02 at 3 seeds, or sps drops > 40% (then equal-wall-clock control wins).
- Cost: distill ~2 h; 2 arms x 11B x 0.43 x 1.4 (wider net) ~ 13 GPU-h per seed; 3 seeds 40 GPU-h;
  eval 3 x 2 x 9600 = 58k games = 14 CPU-h.

### E9. Combine winners (week 3)
- If >= 2 screens confirm, run a 2x2 (or 2x2x2) factorial of the confirmed changes at 3 seeds,
  7B each: 4 cells x 3 seeds x 3.0 = 36 GPU-h; eval 12 x 9600 = 115k games = 29 CPU-h.
- Protein (T/train/sweep/driver.py) is the wrong tool here: it scores single seeds by Elo
  against a pool (train/sweep/scoring.py "league" only) and needs ~50 lines for an all_combos
  scorer; with MDE 0.044 per 2000-game trial it cannot rank effects below 0.05. Use it only if
  E0 finds s_seed <= 0.01 and >= 3 continuous knobs survive screening (12 trials x 4B = 21 GPU-h).

### E10. Long run and release (week 4)
- Best confirmed configuration from the current main for ~150B (65 GPU-h) with EMA gating and
  the E7 hygiene, then a final 2B anneal; gate 8000 games vs f5 and A20 (MDE 0.016 vs the fixed
  reference) plus the exploiter panel. Counterfactual: the constant-LR loop's slope predicts
  +0.07 per 150B before its own end anneal (+0.05), so the bar is "release >= l2-anneal-2 output
  (the 4B anneal the orchestrator runs at turn 100) + 0.10 on the same panel"; +0.03 to +0.10 means
  the changes helped but not more than the loop would have; < +0.03 means the program found nothing
  the loop does not already get.

## 5. The l2 loop

Pause bopl-l2 during weeks 1-2 (after the turn-100 l2-anneal-2 orchestrator has produced its

speed; the loop's measured value is +0.0049 per 10B = +0.019 per week of exclusive GPU, less than

gating and E7 hygiene is the best known use of the GPU). Keep the hourly exploit evals off while

- 2026-10-19 stage 3 code: `--optimizer adam|muon-adamw`, `--weight-decay`, `--adam-lr` landed (all
  off by default; arms in train/research/arms/optimizer.toml). Smoke runs from the l2 main at
  136.0B, 24 workers, constant LR. **Step size:** Muon at 2.43e-4 moves the weights RMS 0.090 x LR
  per update (2 optimizer steps), KL 4.2e-4. Adam's steady state is 0.26 x LR, so Adam at 3e-4
  steps 3.6x Muon and runs KL 4.6e-3, clip 0.03 (11x). Step-matched Adam LR = 2.43e-4 x 0.090 /
  0.26 = 8.4e-5, which gives KL 7.7e-4: Adam spends 1.8x Muon's KL per unit of weight motion
  (KL-matched ~6.2e-5). The l2 batch is 10x the smoke batch, so the ratio may shift at scale.
  **Weight decay:** the main's norm grew linearly, 274.9 -> 347.9 over 136B (d|w|^2/dB ~ 369).
  Decoupled decay removes 2 lr wd |w|^2 per optimizer step at 40.7k steps/B, so wd ~ 1.5e-4
  holds the norm: a per-step shrink of 3.7e-8 (time constant ~670B, 0.9% over a 6B screen).
  Equilibrium norm scales as sqrt(1/wd), and Muon's step size is fixed, so Moonlight's 0.1
  would collapse the norm. A screen-visible wd (~1e-3) acts as an effective LR increase.
  **Ops:** the per-matrix Muon path (BOPL_MUON_FUSED=0, or more than 16 matrices) hangs under
  CUDA-graph capture; v4 stays on the fused path.
