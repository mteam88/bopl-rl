CRN is real for sibling nets. All arms of a program, and every screen program on base 211001, play the same
setups per (endpoint, opponent slot). So the shared part (r) cancels in every arm contrast, and the residual eval
variance of a contrast is 0.57x that of fresh setups. This revises findings log 1b (r ~ 0, measured across
dissimilar nets). The table below credits no CRN until a whole panel endpoint has two nets. The rows "if CRN
r 0.43 holds" show the gain, which is small: eval noise is already below plausible s_seed.

## Verdicts and proposed fixes

- **ff1 is adequately powered for effects >= 0.026 if s_seed <= 0.02.** The +0.02 gate on the estimate caps power
  at 50% for a true +0.02 by construction. More runs or games barely help: 64 runs give 0.024 and 4000 games give
  0.025, both bounded by the gate. Keep the design. Pre-register that ff1 screens for true effects >= +0.026 and
  pre-register the error term. analyze.py pools the 10 three-factor alias contrasts (internal) with E0's s_seed
  (external) by df, giving t with 15 df. **ff1.toml has no exploit-panel opponents, so the REVIEW condition "exploit
  panel loses <= 0.05" cannot be checked.** Add the four [opponents] tables from e0.toml at the anneal endpoint
  (+1600 games/run) before launch.
- **4 vs 4 confirms are underpowered for +0.02 at any s_seed >= 0.01** (MDE 0.023-0.055). Fix at no GPU cost:
  re-score all six E0 control checkpoints on base 221001 (CPU only). Then confirm k_arm vs 6: 8 vs 6 gives 0.023 at
  s_seed 0.015. For a 0.02 MDE at s_seed 0.015 you need 10 seeds per arm, and more eval games cannot replace seeds.
- **I1/I3 at 4 vs 6 have about 52% power at true 0 for the -0.02 margin** (s_seed 0.015). OFFENSE pre-registered
  the fallback for s_seed >= 0.015: use 6 vs 6, or screen at -0.03 and confirm at 6 vs 6. 6 vs 6 at -0.02 is still
  only 0.65. A -0.03 screening margin at 4 vs 6 gives 0.81. Take that fallback, then confirm 8 vs 8 at -0.02
  (0.76).
- **E8 3 vs 3 resolves its +0.04 hypothesis only if s_seed <= 0.015.** Its "falsified if within +-0.02" band misses
  a true 0 in 15% of runs and catches a true +0.04 in 8% at s_seed 0.015 (26% and 13% at 0.02). Its runs are 10B,
  so s_seed may be larger than E0's 6B value (E0's raw2000m endpoint shows the growth). Go to 4 vs 4 if E0's 80%
  UB is above 0.015.
- **Sizing rule caveat:** with 6 seeds the 80% UB of s_seed averages about 1.5x the true value (chi-square, 5 df).
  The PLAN E0 table applied to the UB will usually pick the larger design. That is intended (REVIEW revision 2).
  It means a true 0.015 lands in the "k 4-6 or n 8000" row, and n 8000 does not help (see seeds at infinite
  games).
- **CRN:** keep every compared arm on the same seed base and the same pair count per opponent, so all setups are
  shared. ff1's 250 pairs/opponent are the first 250 of E0's 538, so contrasts against E0 controls should use the
  common pairs. analyze.py's two-way ANOVA does this within a program, but cross-program contrasts aren't
  implemented yet. I1/I3 arms at 2000 games share only 250 of the controls' 538 pairs: give them 4300 games, or
  contrast on the common pairs.
