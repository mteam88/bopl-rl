RESEARCH DESIGN

One clear first question.

Can a recurrent policy learn to survive and win using dash, arrow, and platform with a delayed view?

  • Train on one map; evaluate on two additional maps.
  • Half the games face earlier checkpoints.
  • Win/loss is the main reward, with a small kill/death bonus.
  • Loadouts and cooldowns are public for every player.
